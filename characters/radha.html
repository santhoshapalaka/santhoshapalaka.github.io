📜 Ślokas

Vedanta Desikan [1268 – 1369 CE] · (1)

निरपेक्ष इवैष नीलया रसिकः पालिकया सराधया ।
पृथगत्र किमेतदुच्यते कुहकः कश्चिदसौ कुलस्य नः ।। यादवाभ्युदयः ९.९० ।।

nirapekṣa ivaiṣa nīlayā rasikaḥ pālikayā sarādhayā |
pṛthagatra kimetaducyate kuhakaḥ kaścidasau kulasya naḥ || yādavābhyudayaḥ 9.90 ||

When Kṛṣṇa leaves for Mathurā, the Gopis try to stop Him from leaving. They think "He has so much Vyāmoham on us, despite us belonging to arivondrum illada ayar kulam, what'll happen if He sees navanāgarikanārīmaṇis? You are leaving Nilā, Rādhā, Pālikā behind! Why is it even necessary to name these three separately? He is deceiving us!"

This despair of the Gopis also appears in Viṣṇupurāṇam (which is the basis for Yādavābhyudayam alongside Śrīmadbhāgavatam, Mahābhāratam and Harivaṃśam): मथुरां प्राप्य गोविन्दः कथं गोकुलमेष्यति । नगरस्त्रीकलालापमधु श्रोत्रेण पास्यति ॥ विष्णुपुराणम् ५.१८.१४ ।।

Vedanta Desikan [1268 – 1369 CE] · (2)

देवकी दनुजस्थूणा दिव्यं धाम व्रजाङ्गणम् ।
रमा राधादयश्चेति राशिभेदैर्न भिद्यसे ।। यादवाभ्युदयः १०.७१ ।।

devakī danujasthūṇā divyaṃ dhāma vrajāṅgaṇam |
ramā rādhādayaśceti rāśibhedairna bhidyase || yādavābhyudayaḥ 10.71 ||

(Vasudeva stuti) Regardless of whether He appears in the womb of Devakī, from a pillar, in Paramapadam or in Vrindāvanam, Piratti Amshabhootaiyāna Ramā, Rādhā etc. are His Divyamahishīs. In Tātparyacandrikā (4.5–4.8) Deśikan says: अवतारस्य सत्यत्वं अजहत्स्वस्वभावता — even when He appears in the form of an Avatāram, He doesn't lose his 'thani perum thanmaigazh'. Thus He always appears with Her.

Viṣṇupurāṇam describes how She always accompanies Him: राघवत्वेऽभवत्सीता रुक्मिणी कृष्णजन्मनि । अन्येषु चावतारेषु विष्णोरेषानपायिनी ॥ विष्णुपुराणम् १.९.१४१ ॥

Nammazhvar says that He appears here with the same Thejas as in Paramapadam: ஆதி அம்சோதி உருவை அங்கு வைத்துங்குப் பிறந்த (Thiruvāymozhi 3.5.5).
Deśikan mentions this in Daśāvatārastotram: यद्भावेषु पृथग्विधेष्वनुगुणान् भावान् स्वयं बिभ्रति (दशावतारस्तोत्रम् १)

Ghatikashatamammāḷ [14th Century CE] · (3)

पद्मे त्वन्नयने स्मरामि सततं भावोद्भवत्कुन्तले (भावोऽभवत्कुन्तले)
नीले मुह्यति किं करोमि महितैः क्रीतोऽस्मि ते विभ्रमैः ।
इत्युत्स्वप्नवचो निशम्य स रुषा निर्भर्त्सितो राधया
कृष्णस्तत्परमेव तद्-व्यपदिशन् क्रीडाविटः पातु नः ।। (वेदान्तविलासम्) यतिराजविजयम् १.१ ।।

padme tvannayane smarāmi satataṃ bhāvodbhavatkuntale
nīle muhyati kiṃ karomi mahitaiḥ krīto'smi te vibhramaiḥ |
ityutsvapnavaco niśamya sa ruṣā nirbhartsito rādhayā
kṛṣṇastatparameva tad-vyapadiśan krīḍāviṭaḥ pātu naḥ || (Vedāntavilāsam) Yatirājavijayam 1.1 ||

Whilst Kṛṣṇa was sleeping beside Rādha, He blabbered "Oh Padmā! I am enchanted by the beauty of your eyes. Oh Nīlā Devi! I am captivated by your braid." Rādhā furiously wakes Him and demands an explanation. Kṛṣṇa craftily reassured her by saying that he was in fact referring to Rādhā (and not any other girl) with the names Padmā and Nīlā. परिहासेऽप्यनौचित्यं स्वप्नेऽप्यन्यवधूकथाम् (pratāparudrīya nāṭakam; Kṛṣṇa pretends to follow such a standard). Upon hearing these words from Parama Śṛṃgāra Nāyaka (Kṛṣṇa), Rādha was pacified and she let Him fall asleep in Her lap. May (that) Kṛṣṇa protect us.

This is the invocatory first verse of this play. Appaya Dīkshitar too has quoted this verse.

🖊️ Further Notes

  • Rādha and Kṛṣṇa were married by Brahma in a place known as 'Pāndivanam' (note: different from Bhāndīravanam) in Vrindāvanam, per Garga Saṃhitā.
  • Whilst Śrīmadbhāgavatam doesn't explicitly mention her by name, she has been glorified by our Āchāryas and is hence without a doubt Perumazh's Consort.
